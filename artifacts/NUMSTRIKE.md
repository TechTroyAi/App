## What's Inside Demo v1.0.0

1. **Solo-Leveling Style Status System (`Rank F` → `Rank S`)**
   - Tracks your **CP (Calculation Power)**, **SPD (Average Speed)**, **ACC (Accuracy)**, **CMB (Best Combo)**, and **Boss Table Mastery**.
2. **High-Stakes Wrong Answer Penalty**
   - **Standard Penalty:** `-20 CP` + Combo Reset + Instant **Secret Breakdown Toast** showing how to solve the exact question you missed.
   - **Brutal Mode:** `-40 CP` + `-3.0s` off the clock + Combo Reset.
3. **Mind-Freeze Auto-Whisper (Anti-Blanking Assist)**
   - If you hesitate for more than `2.4 seconds` on any question, the **Secret Whisper Box** automatically lights up with the first mental step (e.g. Base-10 Ghost Gap or 5-Anchor Jump) so you never freeze up.
4. **Interactive Secret Techniques Dojo**
   - Includes a live **Interactive Breakdown Lab** where you can tap numbers and see the step-by-step mental shortcut, plus 1-tap targeted drills for all 6 Secret Techniques.
5. **12×12 Multiplication Speed Matrix & Weakness Assassin**
   - Color-codes every multiplication pair (`<2.1s Gold`, `Learned Cyan`, `Needs Work Red`) and lets you tap any cell to inspect its fastest mental trick.
