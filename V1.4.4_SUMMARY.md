### 🎮 Forge Overdrive Mechanic (NEW)

**What it does:**
- Player-activated combat boost that temporarily enhances ALL towers
- **Fire Rate:** +45% faster (×0.55 interval multiplier)
- **Damage:** +25% bonus damage
- **Duration:** 8 seconds

**How to charge:**
- Normal enemy kill: +4 charge
- Elite kill: +15 charge  
- Boss kill: +35 charge
- Meter capacity: 100 charge points

**How to use:**
- Tap the Overdrive button in the top HUD command cluster (between reset/reforge and primary action)
- Button shows real-time charge progress as a radial fill arc
- Pulses gold when full and ready
- Shows countdown timer while active

**Visual effects:**
- Warm gold glow on all towers while overdrive is active
- Radial charge ring around arcane gear icon
- Pulsing gold highlight when meter is full
- Red-orange countdown ring while active
- Visual burst particles on activation

### 📐 HUD Button Relocation

**New top bar command cluster layout:**

[Overdrive] [Reset/Reforge] [Primary Action] [Sound] [Feedback] [Pause]

The overdrive button is positioned for easy access during combat without leaving the command cluster.

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

**Forge Overdrive creates a risk/reward decision loop:**
- Save it for a boss wave or use it early to clear a swarm?
- The meter charges from kills, so aggressive defense = faster recharge
- Elites and bosses give big chunks, rewarding focused fire
- The 8-second window is long enough to feel impactful but short enough to matter

**Fantasy Machinery HUD sprites reinforce the forge-fantasy aesthetic:**
- Every button feels like a piece of arcane machinery rather than a flat UI element
- Brass, copper, gears, rivets, arcane runes, steampunk-meets-magic
- Consistent visual language across all HUD elements
