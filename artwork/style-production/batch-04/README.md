# Batch 04 — complete regular-enemy family

Integrated original gameplay sprites:

- Runner
- Brute
- Shellback
- Splitling

Together with the approved Mosser prototype, these complete all five regular-enemy visuals. Their silhouettes reinforce gameplay roles at combat scale: Runner is narrow and forward-leaning, Brute is broad and massive, Shellback is low and plated, and Splitling is visibly divided into two fused halves.

The high-resolution `*_source.png` concepts were created specifically for Blockhold Defense. `processed/` contains the transparent 64×64 outputs produced by `tools/process_sprite_batch_04.py`.

`regular-enemy-family-review.png` presents the complete family at enlarged nearest-neighbor scale and is not packaged into the APK.
