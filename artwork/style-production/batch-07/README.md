# Batch 07 — complete corruption family

Integrated original gameplay sprites:

- Carapace Growth
- Hex Bloom
- Blink Root
- Thorn Soil
- Brood Nest

Together with the approved Spore Path prototype, these complete all six terrain-corruption visuals. Each stays low and terrain-bound while communicating its mechanic through armor plates, interference petals, displaced roots, construction thorns, or Splitling cocoons.

The high-resolution `*_source.png` concepts were created specifically for Blockhold Defense. `processed/` contains transparent 64×64 outputs produced by `tools/process_sprite_batch_07.py`. Carapace Growth was revised during review to remove a creature-like silhouette and read clearly as stationary terrain.

`corruption-family-review.png` presents the complete family at enlarged nearest-neighbor scale and is not packaged into the APK.
