# Batch 10 — combat tower evolutions

Integrated original evolution emblems for the three direct-attack towers:

- Chain Conductor
- Rail Spire
- Blizzard Lens
- Shatter Crystal
- Siege Mortar
- Gravity Cannon

Each emblem communicates its gameplay branch as a compact forge-built component: jumping conduction, line piercing, slowing frost, frozen critical damage, extreme bombardment, or gravitational pull. They are upgrade-selection icons rather than alternate full-tower appearances.

The high-resolution `*_source.png` concepts were created specifically for Blockhold Defense. `processed/` contains transparent 64×64 outputs produced by `tools/process_sprite_batch_10.py`.

`combat-evolution-review.png` presents all six emblems at enlarged nearest-neighbor scale and is not packaged into the APK.
