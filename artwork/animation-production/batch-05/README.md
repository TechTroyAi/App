# Animation Batch 05 — remaining traps

Integrated three-frame trigger strips for:

- Ember Rune
- Arc Plate
- Crusher Block

The approved original 64×64 sprites remain unchanged as frame B. These traps do not loop continuously. Their resting battlefield, toolbar, status, and stored-trap appearances use frame A; activation jumps to frame C and recovers through exact neutral B before returning to A.

Ember Rune cools its original engraved channels at rest and flashes the same channel layout at activation. Arc Plate dims its perimeter wiring and lowers its central conductor at rest, then drives a compact cyan surge through the same hardware. Crusher Block opens its two jaws without moving the foundation or piston mounts, then closes the same jaws along a restrained ember-orange impact seam.

Eight successful generation calls were used in the reviewed production session. The initial Crusher poses did not communicate the requested open/closed travel and were replaced; the accepted corrected outputs arrived with their pose semantics reversed, so they were assigned to the matching A and C slots after visual review. Two correction slots remained unused.
